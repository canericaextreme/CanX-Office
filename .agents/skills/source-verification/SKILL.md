# Source Verification

## Trigger and command

Use when application functionality depends on government standards, regulations, contracts, maintenance requirements, geographic boundaries, official documents or other authoritative information. Scope: Prefer official sources, record source/title/version/date where available, verify currency, summarize in original plain language where appropriate and distinguish official information from interpretation. Boundary: Never invent, estimate or silently substitute missing authoritative information. Do not present manually created or inferred information as official.

Invoke explicitly with `/source-verification`.

## Authority

Follow current Workspace Knowledge at all times, and resolve conflicts using the stated project/workspace authority rules. Read relevant project knowledge before acting. This Skill adds no authorization: review/inspect/check/diagnose is read-only; prepare/draft is proposal-only; implement only the owner-authorized scope. Preserve security, privacy, data integrity, evidence/history, reporter loop-back, jurisdiction separation, ownership/portability and human authority over safety-critical AI.

## Workflow

1. Identify the claims and exact jurisdiction or subject requiring authoritative support. Prefer the responsible official publisher and primary documents.
2. Record source URL or document identity, title, issuer, version, publication/effective date and verification date where available. Check amendments, superseded versions and geographic applicability.
3. Summarize in original plain language without changing the meaning. Clearly label official requirements separately from platform interpretation or human inference.
4. Flag unavailable, outdated, conflicting or incomplete evidence and request human review rather than guessing. Never imply government endorsement or unsupported coverage.

## Boundary

Never invent, estimate or silently substitute missing authoritative information. Do not present manually created or inferred information as official.
